A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What should I include in a custom build request?

Include your budget, main games or applications, monitor resolution, and any parts you already own. Mention whether you also need a monitor or peripherals.

Can I upgrade my existing computer?

Choose “Upgrade an existing PC” and list your processor, motherboard, memory, graphics card, power supply, and case. These details help assess potential upgrades.

Does the configurator verify compatibility?

The current configurator is a layout demonstration with sample prices and power figures. Verify sockets, memory support, case dimensions, and power requirements before buying parts.

Will this form send an order?

No. This is a frontend course project. The form checks required fields and displays a local confirmation; it does not transmit or store your information.
